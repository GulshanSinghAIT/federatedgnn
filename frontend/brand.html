FedFairGNN

Models that travel. Data that stays.

A federated graph neural network for privacy-preserving, fairness-audited clinical decision support - built so hospitals learn together without sharing a single patient record.

01 / Foundation

The idea, in one shape

Three separate nodes, kept apart for privacy, connected to one model in the middle. That single picture is the product, the logo, and the promise. Every brand decision points back to it.

Private

Sovereign by default

Raw patient data never leaves the hospital. Only model weights move across the network. The brand should always feel discreet and trustworthy - never extractive.

Fair

Equity is a feature

Outcomes are audited across demographics, not assumed. We surface bias plainly. Color, language, and accessibility all model the fairness we ask the system to deliver.

Connected

Stronger together

A graph of diseases, symptoms, and treatments; a federation of hospitals. The visual language is nodes and edges - relationships made legible at every scale.

02 / Logo

The mark

A central filled node is the aggregated global model. Three outlined satellites are the participating hospitals - outlined, never filled, because their data stays local. The edges are the only thing that flows.

06 / Voice & tone

Plain, precise, accountable

This is clinical software. Copy earns trust by being specific and calm. State what happens, name things as a clinician would, and never hide a limitation behind a friendly phrase.

Say this
“Trained on 3 hospitals · 187 patients. No records left any site.”
“Bias flagged: recall is 9% lower for the 65+ group.”
“Suggested differential - review before acting.”
Not this
“Powered by cutting-edge AI for revolutionary care.”
“Oops! Something went wrong 😕”
“The model is 100% confident in this diagnosis.”
Principles

Write from the clinician's side of the screen - “patients,” not “records in the EHR table.” Numbers belong in mono. Uncertainty is stated, not smoothed over: this is decision support, never a verdict. Errors explain what happened and how to fix it, in the interface's own steady voice - no apologies, no mood.
